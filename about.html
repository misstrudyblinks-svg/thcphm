OUR STORY

From a call to a community.

The Call Prayer Ministry was shaped around a simple conviction: prayer changes people, families and communities. We exist to create a place where people can encounter God, grow in His Word and find genuine spiritual family.

Our journey is not about perfection. It is about responding to God's call one step at a time. Whether you are new to faith, returning to church or searching for a place to belong, there is room for you here.

We believe the church should be a refuge — a place where people are welcomed, strengthened, equipped and sent out to make a difference.

OUR FOUNDATION

What We Believe

These convictions shape the way we worship, serve, teach and live together as a church family.

01

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is Lord and Savior and that through Him we receive forgiveness, new life and reconciliation with God.

02

The Word of God

We believe Scripture is foundational for faith and practice, guiding us toward truth, wisdom and a life that honors God.

03

Prayer

We believe prayer is a vital relationship with God and a powerful way to seek His will, intercede for others and grow in faith.

04

The Church

We believe the church is a spiritual family called to worship together, care for one another and serve the world with love.

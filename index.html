WELCOME HOME

There is always room for you here.

The Call Prayer Ministry is a community built around prayer, worship, God's Word and genuine fellowship. We believe that every person has a purpose and that every journey begins with a step of faith.

Whether you are discovering faith, returning to church, or looking for a spiritual family, we invite you to make this house your place of refuge.
